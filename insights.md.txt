#Telecom Customer Churn — Key Insights

## 1. Overall Churn Analysis

- Total customers who stayed: 5,174 (73.46%)
- Total customers who left: 1,869 (26.54%)

### Insight:
The dataset shows a high churn rate of 26.54%, meaning more than 1 in 4 customers are leaving the service. This indicates a significant retention problem.

---

## 2. Churn by Contract Type

- Month-to-month: 3,875 customers | 1,655 churned | 42.71% churn rate
- One year: 1,473 customers | 166 churned | 11.27% churn rate
- Two year: 1,695 customers | 48 churned | 2.83% churn rate

### Insight:
Month-to-month contracts have the highest churn rate by far. Longer contracts significantly improve customer retention.

---

## 3. Churn by Payment Method

- Electronic check: 2,365 customers | 1,071 churned
- Mailed check: 1,612 customers | 308 churned
- Bank transfer (automatic): 1,544 customers | 258 churned
- Credit card (automatic): 1,522 customers | 232 churned

### Insight:
Customers using electronic checks are most likely to churn, while automated payment methods show lower churn rates.

---

## 4. Tenure Analysis

- 0–1 year: 2,186 customers | 1,037 churned
- 1–2 years: 1,024 customers | 294 churned
- 2–4 years: 1,594 customers | 325 churned
- 4+ years: 2,239 customers | 213 churned

### Insight:
New customers (0–1 year) are most likely to churn. Churn decreases as customer tenure increases, showing stronger loyalty over time.

---

## 5. High-Risk Customers

High-risk customers are those who:
- Have month-to-month contracts
- Pay more than 70 monthly charges
- Have tenure less than 12 months

### Insight:
This segment represents customers most likely to churn and should be targeted with retention strategies such as discounts, incentives, or contract upgrades.

---

## 6. Revenue Impact

- Estimated monthly revenue loss due to churn: 139,130.85

### Insight:
Churn leads to significant financial loss. Even small improvements in retention could result in substantial revenue recovery.

---

## Final Summary

The main drivers of churn are:
- Contract type (month-to-month highest risk)
- Payment method (electronic check highest risk)
- Tenure (new customers most vulnerable)

Retention strategies should focus on early-stage customers and encouraging longer-term contracts.
